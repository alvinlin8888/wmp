寄送刪除帳號要求

也可在 App 內刪除

  1. 登入 WMP，開啟「遊戲設定」→「玩家帳號」。

  3. 閱讀警告後按「刪除帳號」確認。

Delete Your Where's My Pet? Account

This page lets you request deletion of your WMP player account and associated cloud data without reinstalling or opening the app. Service provider: Golden Code AI Studio.

Request deletion outside the app

Email goldencode8888@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in with Google or Apple. Use the subject “Delete my Where's My Pet? account.” If you no longer have access to that address, explain this in your message. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deleting data.

Email an account deletion request

Delete within the app

Sign in, open Game Settings → Player Account, tap Sign Out, select “Delete Player Account And All Information,” then confirm Delete Account.

What is deleted

After verifying your request, we delete the WMP cloud player account and associated player data, such as the player profile, friend links, and world ranking records. Your Google or Apple account is not deleted. Local game progress and device settings are not automatically erased; you may clear the app's data or uninstall it on your device.

If some transaction or security records must be retained by law, we will handle them under our Privacy Policy and explain this in our response. Contact: goldencode8888@gmail.com.
